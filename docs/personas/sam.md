## Sam

* Role: Adult booker
* Background: Sam is an adult who books pottery classes for themself, and
              sometimes for a friend or partner too, on a phone. They have no
              children in any program and expect booking a class to work like
              booking a restaurant table.
* Goals:
    - To pick a class time, choose how many seats, pay, and be done in a few
      minutes.
    - To know before paying what happens if plans change: whether money comes
      back and how late they can reschedule.
    - To reschedule without calling the studio, when the terms allow it.
* Challenges:
    - Little patience for creating an account before seeing a price.
    - Losing a seat because payment took too long.
    - Terms that are hard to find or written in legal language.
