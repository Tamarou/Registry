## Theo

* Role: Front-desk staff
* Background: Theo works the front desk at a pottery studio on class days.
              He greets students, checks who is booked into each class, and
              answers the phone when someone needs to move a booking. He does
              not set prices or publish offers; that is the owner's job.
* Goals:
    - To see today's classes, how many seats are taken, and who is coming, at
      a glance on a tablet at the desk.
    - To mark who showed up when there is time, and skip it when there is not.
    - To reschedule a customer's booking for them, within the studio's terms.
* Challenges:
    - A busy desk: walk-ins, the phone and the counter sale all at once.
    - Classes that start early or late in the day, which must show on the
      right day.
    - Not being able to bend the terms, and having to say so politely.
