## Rosa

* Role: Studio owner and admin
* Background: Rosa owns a small pottery studio that runs drop-in and weekly
              wheel and hand-building classes for adults. She sets the
              studio's prices and terms herself: sales are final, and a
              booking can be rescheduled up to 48 hours before for a small
              fee. She sells clay, glazes and drinks at the counter through
              her own point of sale, and handles refunds and disputes in her
              own Stripe dashboard.
* Goals:
    - To publish a class, its seats and its offers once, and schedule a
      season of occurrences without re-entering prices.
    - To have every booking follow the terms she set, without exceptions to
      remember at the counter.
    - To get paid directly into her own Stripe account.
* Challenges:
    - Little time for software; setup has to be done between classes.
    - Keeping online bookings, counter sales and her Stripe dashboard telling
      the same story.
    - Explaining "sales are final" to customers who did not read the terms.
